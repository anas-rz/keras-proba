    Examples¶

    End-to-end examples on real datasets, as notebooks: read them here, or run them on Google Colab with one click. Each one explains a single uncertainty technique, applied either during training or after training (post-processing).

    Tasks¶

    • Forecasting: Forecast the temperature 24 hours ahead: at a weather station from its last 48 hours of measurements, or everywhere on Earth with a pre-trained global weather model. (4 examples)
    • Regression: Predict house values in California districts from census data. (2 examples)
    • Classification: Classify clothing images, and recognize inputs unlike the training data (handwritten digits). (4 examples)
